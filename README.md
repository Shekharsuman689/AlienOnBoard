AOB – Alien On Board 🌿
Portable Nanocellulose Air Purifier

AOB (Alien On Board) is a portable personal air purifier designed to provide clean air in pollution-heavy environments. The device uses nanocellulose filtration technology combined with activated carbon to remove airborne pollutants while maintaining sustainability.

The product aims to create a personal clean air zone for users traveling, working in offices, or living in polluted urban areas.

Product Overview

Air pollution is a major issue in countries like India, especially in cities such as Delhi, Kolkata, and Mumbai. AOB addresses this challenge by using biodegradable nanocellulose filters that can capture fine pollutants while reducing environmental waste compared to traditional synthetic filters.

The purifier is designed to be:

Portable and travel friendly

Battery operated

USB-C rechargeable

Compact and lightweight

Environmentally sustainable

It provides a clean air bubble around the user rather than purifying an entire room.

What is Nanocellulose?

Nanocellulose is an ultra-fine material made from plant cellulose such as:

Wood pulp

Cotton

Agricultural waste

Key characteristics:

Biodegradable

Lightweight

Extremely strong

Nano-scale fibers

Because of its extremely small fibers, nanocellulose forms a dense filtration network capable of capturing microscopic particles.

How Nanocellulose Cleans Air

Nanocellulose works as a high-efficiency filtration medium.

It can trap:

Dust particles (PM2.5, PM10)

Bacteria

Pollen

Smoke particles

The ultra-fine fiber structure allows the filter to capture pollutants more efficiently than many traditional materials.

With chemical modification, nanocellulose can also:

Absorb harmful gases like formaldehyde

Capture heavy metals from emissions

Bind volatile organic compounds (VOCs)

Multi-Layer Filtration System

AOB uses a multi-layer filtration structure similar to modern air purifiers.

Layer 1 — Pre Filter

Captures large particles such as:

Hair

Dust

Lint

Layer 2 — Nanocellulose Filter

Traps very fine airborne particles including:

PM2.5 pollution

Allergens

Smoke particles

Layer 3 — Activated Carbon Layer

Removes:

Odors

Harmful gases

VOCs

This layered design improves filtration performance while maintaining airflow efficiency.
